( The Studio )

Albanian roots,
Italian craft

"Top Line" shpk is one of the first Albanian-Italian companies dedicated to the import and complete furnishing of homes and offices — for private clients, companies and public entities. Active since 2003, what began in Tirana now reaches Albania's most prestigious coastal residences.

Our showroom pairs dynamic room displays with the catalogues of Italy's most respected manufacturers, and our own architects and installation technicians carry every project from the first sketch to the final fitting.

« Top Line mund të projektojë, realizojë dhe të kompletojë mobilimin e shtëpive dhe zyrave. »
